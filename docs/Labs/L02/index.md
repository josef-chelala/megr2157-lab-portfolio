## Design Rule 
Most printers perform well with walls that fall within typical ranges of 1 to 3 millimeters. The ideal thickness depends on the specific material and process being used.
https://avidpd.com/additive-manufacturing/design-for-additive-manufacturing-5-rules-every-engineer-should-know/
  
## FDM Consideration
The safe rule of thumb for overhangs are at a 45 degree angel. 3D printers are imperfect, and some 3d print jobs will look ugly or completely fail once past the 45 degree rule. This can be fixed by adding supports to the print job.
[https://3dplatform.com/blogs/blog/printing-overhangs-beyond-45-degrees](https://sovol.eu/blogs/new/45-degree-rule-3d-printing-overhangs-support-quality-guide)
